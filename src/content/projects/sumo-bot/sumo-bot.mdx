# UMIEEE SumoBot


UMIEEE/UMRT Sumobot competed in [RoboWars 2026](https://www.robowars.ca/), a competition hosted by IEEE Concordia in Montreal, QB.

The main controller is an ESP32-S3 using both cores for sensors and control logic.
The bot uses a mixture of IR sensors underneath the scoop and facing forward to identify the sumo ring perimeter and the opponent sumobot. 
The controller also drives the two left and right motor drivers, and perform our sumobot's strategies.

<Image src={system_diagram} alt="System Diagram" class="mx-auto w-100" />

UMIEEE was able to qualify for the final tournament and finished in the top 12 of about 23 teams.
