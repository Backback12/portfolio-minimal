# UMIEEE SumoBot


UMIEEE/UMRT Sumobot competed in [RoboWars 2026](https://www.robowars.ca/) Competition hosted by IEEE Concordia in Montreal, QB.

The SumoBot uses a mixture of IR sensors underneath the scoop and facing forward to identify the sumo ring as well as the opponent sumobot. 

The main controller is an ESP32-S3 to control the motor drivers, process the sensors, and perform our sumobot's strategies.


UMIEEE was able to qualify for the final tournament and finished in the top 12 of about 23 teams.
