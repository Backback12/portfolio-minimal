# UMIEEE SumoBot

UMIEEE/UMRT Sumobot competed in the 2026 RoboWars Competition hosted by IEEE Concordia in Montreal, QB.

The SumoBot uses a mixture of IR sensors at underneath the scoop and in front to identify the sumo ring as well as track the opponent sumobot. 

The microcontroller was an ESP32-S3 which was able to control the motor drivers and read the sensors on two different cores for optimal computing.


UMIEEE was able to qualify for the final tournament and finished in the top 12 of about 23 teams.
