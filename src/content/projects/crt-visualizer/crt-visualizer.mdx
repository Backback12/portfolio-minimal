# Audio signal DC bias circuit
Audio signals for this use case use an RMS voltage of 1.0 V, giving peak voltages of** ±1.414 V**. 
For the ESP32 to safely read these signals, a DC bias circuit shifts the signal to a 1.65 V center point, 
for a safe final peak to peak range from ** 0.24 V to 3.06 V.**
<Image src={audio_input_schem} alt="Audio input schematic" class="mx-auto" />
Note that this circuit is effectively a high-pass filter with a cut-off frequency at 31.8 Hz, 
so the audio signals are not perfectly extracted.
